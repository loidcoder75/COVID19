Preventative Measures

Simple actions to protect yourself, your family, and your community.

Hand Hygiene

Wash hands frequently with soap and water for at least 20 seconds or use a 60%+ alcohol-based sanitizer.

Wear a Mask

Wear well-fitted masks in crowded indoor spaces or poorly ventilated areas to reduce transmission risk.

Social Distancing

Maintain safe physical distancing from individuals who show symptoms of respiratory infection.

Recognize the Symptoms

Be aware of early indicators and know when to seek testing or isolate.

Fever & Chills

A persistent high body temperature above 100.4°F (38°C) accompanied by shivering or chills.

Dry Cough & Fatigue

A continuous dry cough accompanied by generalized body weakness and unusual tiredness.

Loss of Smell / Taste

Partial or complete loss of smell (anosmia) or taste, often occurring without a blocked nose.
